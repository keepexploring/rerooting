Summary: Join us as we tour the UK and overseas with screenings of a new independent documentary, exploring pilgrimage, community and creation.

Re-Rooting explores the Christian's relationship with creation by following a
1,000 mile cycle journey undertaken by two friends — Tze-Hung Seeto and Joel
Chaney — from Holy Island in England to Orkney, an archipelago off the north
coast of Scotland.

Following ancient and new pilgrim routes, the journey connects the stories of
individuals and groups whose faith has transformed how they live: from crofters
in the Hebrides to organic farmers in the Orkney isles; from fellow pilgrims to
communities committed to a rhythm and a Way of Life.

## The question behind the film

> What does the Christian faith have to do with how we live in the natural
> world?
>
> For decades we have been hearing news of climate change, environmental
> destruction and the ever diminishing window of opportunity that we have in
> which to act. For much of the time, the Church has been largely silent in
> this conversation, yet the Bible and the gospel of Jesus holds both the
> deepest truth about the purpose of creation and how we are meant to live in
> it; and the greatest hope for the future of the world as well.

— Tze-Hung Seeto, director

## Pilgrimage

The film also explores the ancient Christian tradition of pilgrimage in its
many forms: as the way of devotion, the way of perseverance and, perhaps, even
the way of wisdom. Pilgrimage is by nature a mirror to our spiritual lives
while remaining inextricably rooted in the very soil beneath our feet, and in
that it can help heal the deeply ingrained dualism we create between our
spiritual and material lives.

## What we hope it does

Re-Rooting presents these stories to encourage us not only to take better care
of creation, but also to partake in the abundant gift that creation is to us
all when we live in right, restored relationship with it. The Christian should
have the most to say about the redemption and restoration of the world, not the
least.

## Who made it

**Tze-Hung Seeto** is a documentary photographer and filmmaker who has been
living and working around the world since 2008. His work focusses on the broad
themes of world Christianity, creation care and the experience of war and
conflict, and he runs Wildris Media.

**Joel Chaney** is an innovator and co-founder of CREATIVenergie, a charitable
company that exists to unlock sustainable energy for abundant life.
