## Hosting a screening

We can send you the film, a short introduction to read out, and some questions
to get people talking. It plays well in churches, village halls, community
centres and festivals. It plays best when you leave time afterwards for people
to talk to each other.

Tell us where you are, who you have in mind and roughly when, and we'll take it
from there.
