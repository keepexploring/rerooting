Join us for an Edinburgh screening of the independent documentary 'Re-Rooting:
Pilgrimage, Community & Creation', followed by an interactive Q&A with director
Tze-Hung Seeto and co-pilgrim Joel Chaney.

This event is part of a tour of screenings across the UK and overseas. Watch
the trailer and reserve your seat to join us.

Doors open at 6.30pm for a 7pm start. The film runs about 70 minutes, and we'll
be finished by 9pm.

Entry is free. There will be a collection on the way out, towards the cost of
getting the film to more places.
